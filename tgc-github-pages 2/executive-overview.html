Retail Portal · Executive Overview

Turn our best budtenders into a measurable sales engine

A mobile portal where Certified Dealers log verified sales of The Grower Circle, earn capped monthly product rewards, and give us clean, trustworthy sell-through data — starting with a Las Vegas pilot.

Version 1 · Certified Dealer Program · Las Vegas

What it is

One simple loop that rewards the people who actually move our product.

Budtender sells TGC → Logs the sale + proof → Admin approves → Earns XP → Unlocks monthly reward

Why Certified Dealers worked before

We're formalizing something that already drove reorders.

Budtenders decide the sale

Shoppers ask "what's good?" The budtender's recommendation is the point of sale. Rewarding them moves volume.

Relationships → reorders

Engaged budtenders keep TGC top-of-mind and stocked, which pulls the store into repeat orders.

What was missing

Proof and structure. Version 1 adds verified tracking so the program is fair, measurable, and hard to game.

How Version 1 tracks verified sales

Proof on every sale

Each submission requires a receipt photo or approved proof. Nothing counts without evidence.

Fraud & duplicates reviewed, not accused

The system quietly flags possible duplicates — same receipt, store, date, time, product, a reused photo, or the same transaction claimed by two people — and holds them for a human. It never auto-rejects or accuses.

How XP and rewards work

Simple, capped, and fully in your control.

10 XP

per approved unit — one flat, admin-set number. XP is awarded only after approval.

Monthly product tiers

  • 10 sales → 5 prerolls
  • 25 → Premium eighth
  • 50 → Premium eighth + Flight Bites
  • 75 → Choice reward
  • 100 → Platinum Mystery Box + merch entry
  • 150+ → Elite Mystery Box + VIP entry

Capped by design

Monthly sales reset each month; a dealer earns the highest tier they reach — so your reward budget is predictable. Lifetime totals never reset, for long-term recognition.

How fulfillment is tracked

Physical rewards, picked up locally in Las Vegas — no shipping in Version 1.

Earned → Prepared → Ready for pickup → Picked up

Every reward records who fulfilled it and when. A "reward liability" number shows what's earned but not yet handed out, so nothing slips.

Why this helps sell-through and reorders

More recommendations

Budtenders have a direct, tangible reason to put TGC in the bag.

Clean data

Verified, de-duplicated sales by store, brand, and product — so we know exactly what's moving where.

Smarter reorders

That data tells reps which stores to revisit and which products to push, tightening the reorder cycle.
